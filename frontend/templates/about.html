Mission & Safety Architecture

AI-Assisted Triage Safety Foundations

Bridging rural frontline sub-centers to secondary healthcare command through structured, human-in-the-loop medical AI.

Fundamental Clinical Safety Principle

This platform is an AI-assisted information-structuring and handoff platform. It never attempts autonomous medical diagnosis, prescribing medications, or replacing certified healthcare professionals.

All clinical summaries, extracted entities, and safety flags serve solely as an advisory dossier. A licensed medical practitioner makes the final clinical assessment and referral decision.

Zero-Hallucination Policy

Chief clinical concern synthesis is strictly grounded in verbatim patient statements. The model is constrained from inferring non-reported conditions or assuming clinical symptoms not confirmed by the patient.

Transparent Provenance Tags

Every parameter in the clinical packet displays its exact origin badge: Patient Spoken Voice, Frontline Health Worker Measured, AI Structured Extraction, or Deterministic Rule Engine Trigger.

Deterministic Safety Rules

Critical physiological thresholds (SpO2 < 92%, high-grade pyrexia, hypertensive spikes, acute asthma dyspnea) are computed using deterministic logic rules rather than probabilistic LLM inference.

Standardized 15-Section Dossier

Converts intake data into a structured dossier formatted for instant A4 printing and PDF export, ensuring critical parameters are never omitted during emergency referrals.
