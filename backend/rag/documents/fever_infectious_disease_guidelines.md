# Clinical Protocol: Acute Febrile Illness, Sepsis Screening & Vector-Borne Diseases

## 1. High Pyrexia & Systemic Inflammatory Response
- **Definition**: Core body temperature >= 38.3°C (101°F) or hypothermia <= 36.0°C.
- **Sepsis Screening (qSOFA Criteria)**:
  - Respiratory Rate >= 22 breaths/min
  - Altered mentation (Glasgow Coma Scale < 15)
  - Systolic Blood Pressure <= 100 mmHg
  - **Action**: Any patient with fever meeting >= 2 qSOFA criteria must be triaged as `CRITICAL` for septic shock risk. Immediate blood cultures, IV crystalloid resuscitation (30 mL/kg), and broad-spectrum empiric antibiotics within the "Golden Hour".

## 2. Tropical Vector-Borne Infections (Dengue, Malaria, Typhoid)
- **Dengue Warning Signs**:
  - Persistent vomiting and severe abdominal pain/tenderness.
  - Mucosal bleeding (epistaxis, gingival bleed, hematuria).
  - Clinical fluid accumulation (pleural effusion, ascites).
  - Lethargy, restlessness, or hepatomegaly > 2 cm.
  - Hematocrit surge coinciding with rapid drop in platelet count (< 100,000 /uL).
- **Severe Malaria (Plasmodium falciparum)**:
  - Cerebral symptoms, seizures, dark urine ("blackwater fever"), respiratory distress, or severe anemia (Hb < 7 g/dL).
  - Immediate parenteral Artesunate regimen and higher-center referral.
